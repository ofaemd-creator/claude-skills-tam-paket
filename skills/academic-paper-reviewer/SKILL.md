## Workflow

Review journal fit, three complementary domain/method/statistical perspectives and a skeptical alternative-explanation pass. Anchor every finding in a manuscript location, evidence, consequence and remedy. Distinguish fatal, major, minor and unresolved questions. Verify revisions and expose shared blind spots: five roles in one model are not independent reviewers. Do not impersonate editors, issue real editorial decisions or invent calibrated accuracy scores. No agents are spawned unless separately authorized.

No hooks, APIs, executables, fonts, models or runtime dependencies are installed by this skill. ## Workflow

Identify article type, research question, target journal and verified data. Plan sections, methods, results, argument, references, reporting requirements, declarations and author decisions. Use role-separated drafting and checking passes within the current conversation; multiple passes are not independent human review. Never invent results or citations. Return a draft, claim-source ledger, consistency checks, missing evidence and revision plan. Upstream agents, scripts and automatic hooks are not bundled or enabled.
